Our Story

From the decks of superyachts to the peaks of Colorado — a quarter century of caring for people, and the home that started it all.

Where It Began

A Cabin Called Tuckaway

Tuckaway was the cottage Thomas’s great-grandfather built in Canada — the place his childhood summers were spent. The name, and everything it stood for, was passed down.

In the summer of 2015, Thomas and Tina fulfilled a lifelong dream: a small mountain cabin in Colorado. They named it Tuckaway. When their second daughter, Aven Grace, arrived the next spring, weekends there grew scarce — so rather than leave it empty, they began sharing it with guests.

What began as a simple decision quickly became a revelation. Guests loved staying at Tuckaway — they felt something deeper. The thoughtful design, the comfort, the small details, and the genuine care behind every preparation created a true home-away-from-home. Reviews poured in. Guests returned. Word spread.

All along, we were caring for the people inside the home.
