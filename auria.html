The Residence

Auria sits where the trees open onto Peak 8 — close enough that you click in at the door and ski to the lift, high enough that the evening light lands on the great room before it reaches the valley.

Eight bedrooms and nine and a half baths mean a family of twenty-four sleeps well and wakes without a queue. The living spaces are new, warm, and quietly grand: soaring glass to the mountain, stone and timber that feel like Colorado rather than a catalogue, and a kitchen built for the kind of long dinner nobody wants to end.

It is the largest and most exclusive home in our care, and it is prepared the way we prepare every home — by hand, in person, before you arrive.
