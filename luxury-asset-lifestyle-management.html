Redefining ownership.
Preserving legacy.

Experience a new standard in luxury home stewardship — an invitation-only program crafted for Summit County’s most distinguished residences.

Request a Private Consultation Discover the Program

In a landscape where exceptional homes deserve exceptional care, Hosting from the Heart proudly introduces its Luxury Asset & Lifestyle Management Division — an elite, invitation-only program crafted for the most discerning homeowners in Summit County.

This exclusive offering is limited to a select portfolio of homes that embody architectural distinction, refined craftsmanship, and timeless design. Each property becomes part of a curated collection — managed with the precision of an estate manager, the warmth of a five-star host, and the foresight of a trusted financial advisor.

We don’t simply manage homes — we curate experiences, preserve legacies, and protect value.

By Invitation Only

A curated collection, managed as a private estate.

Owning a luxury mountain retreat should feel effortless. Our mission is to transform ownership into a seamless, rewarding, and worry-free experience — where every arrival feels like a private five-star escape, and every detail reflects the highest standard of hospitality and operational care.

A comprehensive, heart-centered approach

Your residence overseen with the diligence of a private estate. Five-star hosting and bespoke concierge for owners and guests alike. Premium short and mid-term income, with your personal use always protected. Clear reporting and disciplined operations you can always trust.

Each home is treated as an individual masterpiece — cared for, protected, and optimized with the diligence of a private estate or family office asset.

Every HFTH partnership begins with a deep understanding of your property as both a residence and an asset. Our Asset Protection Plan ensures longevity, safety, and performance through:

  • Comprehensive home inspection and systems evaluation
  • Preventative maintenance scheduling and oversight
  • Detailed vendor management and performance tracking
  • 24/7 emergency response and incident reporting
  • Annual property condition and capital forecast report
  • Full compliance review for insurance, safety, and regulations

We operate with absolute accountability — safeguarding your home as if it were our own.

100%
Asset Protection Guarantee

Full asset-protection insurance shields every homeowner from damage, replacement costs, or loss — paired with transparent financial reporting delivered by the fifteenth of every month.

Because every property, and every owner, is unique, HFTH develops a personalized Luxury Asset & Lifestyle Plan for each home. Our process includes:

  1. IA comprehensive home inspection and operational analysis.
  2. IIA detailed lifestyle questionnaire to understand preferences, travel patterns, and your desired level of involvement.
  3. IIICreation of a customized management and lifestyle plan.
  4. IVA tailored revenue projection and operating cost analysis for rental potential.

Every recommendation is aligned with your vision, lifestyle, and financial objectives.
